## 4. Simulation settings and mesh

* Unit cell 825 × 825 nm, periodic (Bloch k = 0) in x and y; PML (16 cells) at both z ends; normal incidence from air, E ∥ x, `UniformPlaneSource` (TFSF, one-directional −z), Gaussian pulse centred at 1300 nm with σ_f = 13 THz (σ_t = 12.2 fs, peak at t = 73 fs; amplitude ≥ 0.3 over 1200–1400 nm). Identical source, grid and DFT settings for all structures and for the empty reference run.
* Grid: rectilinear. In-plane 64 × 64 cells of 12.891 nm (2 × 2 design pixels; the a-Si:H pattern enters through the exact area fill fraction of every cell with FDTDX sub-pixel smoothing = True; 3.6 % of the cells are interface cells for final3).
  z: 154 cells — glass 1726 nm (PML 412 nm + bulk, 25.8-nm cells, graded to the ITO), ITO **5 cells of 4.60 nm**, a-Si:H graded 4.6 → 12.9 nm then 12.9-nm cells (h exact), air graded to 12.9 nm; source 296 nm above the a-Si:H top, R plane 129 nm above the source (scattered-field region), T plane 334 nm below the ITO in the glass, air PML 103 nm above the R plane.
* Time step 13.01 as = 0.95 × the exact 3D CFL limit of the rectilinear grid; 23053 steps = 300 fs (with ITO); no-ITO runs 69159 steps = 900 fs (a first 450-fs final3 no-ITO record is archived as `final3/noito450`). DFT phasors recorded every 19 steps (0.247 fs sampling, ≥ 8× the Nyquist rate of the pulse), pulse scaling (no window). Spectra on 1200–1400 nm every 2 nm (101 wavelengths); volume/plane field phasors at λ = 1302.28, 1294.00, 1298.00, 1306.00, 1310.00 nm.
* Detectors: `R_plane`, `T_plane` (Ex, Ey, Hx, Hy phasors → ½Re(E×H*)·dA integrated over the cell), `vol_fields` (Ex, Ey, Ez phasors over 4 glass cells + ITO + a-Si:H + 4 air cells), `xz_plane`/`yz_plane` (full height through the cell centre), `probe_asi`/`probe_ito` (time-domain field probes for the decay check). Reference run: same domain filled with air, `T_plane` → incident spectrum P_inc(λ), `R_plane` → TFSF backward leakage (≤ 3×10⁻⁷ of P_inc), `inc_field_plane` → incident Ex phasor for field normalization.
* Quantities: T = −S_z(T plane)/P_inc, R = (S_z(R plane) − leakage)/P_inc, A = 1 − R − T; E/E_inc = phasor / incident Ex phasor (same complex normalization for all structures); F_c = (ω/c) Im ε_ITO(λ) ∫_ITO |E_c/E_inc|² dV / P² (FDTD analogue of the TORCWA ITO loss components); U_c(z) = ∫∫|E_c/E_inc|² dx dy.

* Superseded configuration (kept for the record, not used): a first final3 run with only 350 nm of glass below the ITO gave T < 0 around 1262–1290 nm and A = 1.002 at 1286 nm, because the (±1, 0) diffraction orders are evanescent in the glass only just below their cut-off (n_glass·P = 1251 nm; decay length ≈ 475 nm at λ_ZE, ≈ 1 µm at 1262 nm) and reached the PML. The production mesh uses 1.25 µm of bulk glass + a 412-nm PML. Residual near-cut-off artefacts (1251–1265 nm) are still expected to be larger than elsewhere.

## 8. Warnings, limitations, failed items

* No GPU in the session: CPU-only execution forced a coarser production grid (64 cells per period, 12.9-nm cells with sub-pixel smoothing, 5 ITO cells) than a GPU run would use; the ITO z-refinement check quantifies the mesh sensitivity, the in-plane resolution was not varied.
* Near the glass Rayleigh cut-off (1251 nm) the (±1,0) orders are grazing/evanescent with decay lengths up to microns; values within ~1251–1265 nm carry a larger PML-related uncertainty (see §4).
* Multipole moments use the vacuum long-wavelength Cartesian formulas with the origin at the a-Si:H centre; the a-Si:H sits on ITO/glass in a periodic lattice, so the 'radiated power' weights are diagnostics only. Partial interface cells are weighted by their exact fill fraction.
* The 'main resonance' field maps use the recorded field wavelength closest to the A-maximum of the spectrum (recorded set 1294/1298/1302.28/1306/1310 nm with ITO; 1280–1320 nm every 10 nm without ITO); the mismatch is stated in `field_diagnostics.json` (`_mismatch_nm`).
* Without ITO all media are lossless: A_noITO is a numerical residual (flux-plane closure), reported as such.
* No-ITO structures support long-lived modes (TORCWA shows 2-4-nm-wide features near 1252-1275 nm, decay times of a few hundred fs). A first 450-fs no-ITO record (final3/noito450, kept) gave R up to 1.07 and A = -0.02 from DFT truncation; the production no-ITO runs use 900 fs (spectral resolution ~ 1/T = 1.1 THz ~ 6 nm), so features narrower than ~6 nm remain broadened/under-resolved and the lossless closure residual A_noITO is reported as the truncation error indicator.
* With-ITO runs (300 fs): the time-domain probes end at 0.7-4 % of their peak amplitude (comparison/decay_check.json); the corresponding truncation error on the broad (~100-nm) ITO-loaded features is at the sub-percent level (T_min values of -0.0003 to -0.005 give its scale).
* In-plane discretisation (64 cells per period, 12.9 nm, sub-pixel smoothed) is coarser than the 6.4-nm design pixel; only the ITO z-resolution was varied (5 vs 10 cells). Differences to TORCWA of ~0.03-0.05 in A at lambda_ZE and a ~10-15-nm blue-shift of the absorption maximum are within what this in-plane resolution can produce; no in-plane refinement run was affordable on CPU.
* FDTDX pulse-mode phasors are stride-weighted time-step sums; runs with different dt (the 10-cell ITO mesh) are made comparable by multiplying every phasor by dt in fx_post.py (documented in the code).
