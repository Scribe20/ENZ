## 4b. Interpretation and caveats (post hoc)

- **Where the absorption comes from.** At normal incidence the winner's ITO field is only 34% longitudinal (eta_z; the four resonant references average 78%) while its Ez enhancement F_Ez = 2.15 is comparable to theirs (2.24). The ~2.2x higher A_ITO (0.451 vs ~0.20) therefore comes mostly from IN-PLANE fields in the ITO under a high-fill (64% of the cell) 200-nm a-Si slab perforated by narrow air slots, i.e. the ITO acts as a lossy layer loaded by a slab/slot resonance, not primarily through the longitudinal ENZ (Berreman-type) channel. eta_z rises to 0.67 at (15 deg, 90 deg), 0.66 at (30 deg, 90 deg), 0.76 at (20 deg, 45 deg) where the TE-plane and diagonal incidences drive Ez at the slot edges.
- **Resonances.** The loaded r/t pole nearest the ENZ band is at 1499.8 nm with Q_loaded = 5.51 (Q_rad = 50, Q_nr = 6.0); a second certified in-window pole at 1437.6 nm with Q = 74 sits within a few nm of lambda_E and carries the largest Lorentzian peak fraction (r 0.69, t 0.35): a narrow lattice/guided-mode resonance of the P = 800 nm slab (G10/k0 = 1.79 vs K_ENZ/k0 = 1.69) hybridised with the ENZ film. The no-ITO control certified no in-window r/t pole under the significance criteria, so the ITO participates in setting the resonance rather than merely loading a photonic mode.
- **Fabrication sensitivity.** A uniform +-1 px (6.2 nm) edge shift lowers J from 0.353 to 0.277 (-1px) / 0.254 (+1px); +-2 px gives 0.240 / 0.210. The minimum air gap is 31 nm (the realized padding ring itself), the minimum a-Si feature 144 nm. The design is therefore edge-critical at the 6-nm pixel level; a robustness-aware (eroded/dilated) objective would be the natural next step if fabrication tolerance matters.
- **Order convergence.** J changes by < 1% from [7,7] to [11,11], but individual TE-plane angles move by up to 0.06 (e.g. (15 deg, 90 deg): 0.438 -> 0.524); the narrow slots need higher orders for per-angle accuracy.
- **Angular behaviour.** The winner stays above 0.40 across 0-30 deg in the TM plane and above 0.34 in the TE plane (dense mean 0.413), but falls to 0.242 on the 45-deg diagonal at 30 deg; the runner-up (P = 925, h = 240) is flatter at large angles but weaker in the TE plane. Both from-scratch Stage-4 runs at the same cell reached only J ~ 0.21-0.22, so the outer (P, h, pad) screen and warm start, not the seed, produced the result.
